About Charlene

Charlene is a basket weaver who loves the blend of artistry and practicality—pieces that feel good to hold, use, and keep. Her teaching style is friendly, clear, and confidence-building.

Want a custom workshop for a birthday, retreat, or team offsite? Reach out and Charlene will help shape a weaving experience that fits your group.
